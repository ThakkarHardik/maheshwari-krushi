## 1. Market size

- The Indian agritech market is valued at about **$974 million (2025)** and is projected to reach **$2.52 billion by 2034**, growing about 10.6% a year.
- Online agri-input retail (seeds, fertilisers, crop protection, plant tonics) is still a small share of total agri-input sales. Most farmers buy from their local *krishi kendra*. That gap is the opportunity for a trusted local store that also sells online.

## 3. Local context (Sailana / Ratlam, Malwa region)

- Farmers are Hindi-speaking, so the website must be **Hindi-first**. The client's design is already fully in Hindi.
- Major crops in the region: soybean (Kharif); wheat and gram (Rabi); vegetables. Cotton, fodder (jowar) and okra seeds are in the client's starting catalogue.
- **Animal fodder seed (पशु चारा)** is a separate category in the design. Dairy and livestock are important locally.
- Delivery: most early orders will come from Sailana, Ratlam and nearby villages. Same-day or next-day local delivery is a strong advantage over national platforms.

## 4. Customer behaviour to design for

- **COD and UPI/QR dominate.** Card payments are a smaller share. The design offers UPI/QR, card and COD.
- **WhatsApp is the main channel.** Many farmers will ask before they buy. That's why the design's flow sends anyone who isn't ready to buy to Enquiry/WhatsApp.
- **No-login checkout:** the design asks only for name, mobile and address. Forcing a login loses orders.
- Phones are low-end and the internet is slow, so pages must be light and fast.
- Trust signals matter. The design shows "सुरक्षित पेमेंट · बेहतर क्वालिटी · त्वरित डिलीवरी · 100% भरोसा", star ratings, licence number and GSTIN.

## 7. Legal & compliance checklist

- [ ] **Licence No. 890:** confirm the type (seed / fertiliser / insecticide) and the expiry date. Sell online only the product types the licence covers.
  - Seed licence: State Agriculture Department.
  - Fertiliser licence: Fertiliser Control Order (FCO).
  - Insecticide licence: Insecticides Act 1968 / Rules 1971. It is valid only for the products endorsed on it and only within approved areas. CropLife India has raised concerns about unauthorised online pesticide sales, so stricter rules are likely.
- [x] **GSTIN 23ACRPM5603Q1Z2:** GST invoices with HSN codes; CGST + SGST inside MP, IGST outside MP.
- [ ] **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020:** seller name and address, grievance officer, return/refund and shipping policies shown on the site.
- [ ] **DPDP Act:** privacy policy and consent for storing customer mobile numbers.
- [ ] **DLT registration:** required for sending OTP or transactional SMS in India.
- [ ] Show licence no., batch no., manufacturing date and expiry on product pages and invoices.
